# Growth Evidence and Reactivation

Produce an evidence-backed acquisition and reactivation package adapted to the business. Support direct analysis of authorized sources or one consolidated request for the user's agents. Do not claim live verification when only summaries were supplied.

## Configure

Infer business, business model, priority offer, market/language, objectives, systems, available assets, and requested outputs from context. Ask only for missing inputs that block useful work; record other assumptions and proceed.

Default to the last 90 complete days ending yesterday for channel comparisons and a separate 12-month view for seasonality and retention. Respect requested periods. Record exact dates, business timezone, currency, account/store, attribution settings, filters and source freshness. Keep businesses and tenants separate.

Adapt the model:
- Commerce: paid orders, contribution per order, product mix and repurchase.
- Services: qualified leads, booked/attended appointments, won work, collected revenue, capacity and rebooking. Distinguish lead cost from customer acquisition cost.
- Subscriptions: paid activation, recurring revenue, cancellation/reactivation, retained cohorts, contribution and payback. Do not substitute speculative lifetime value for observed contribution.
- Long sales cycles: distinguish pipeline from realized revenue and account for conversion lag.

## Choose the mode

- Agent request: Read [evidence-pack](references/evidence-pack.md) and deliver one self-contained, copy-ready request tailored to actual systems. Sending it to bots or people requires user authorization.
- Evidence audit: Use that reference to inspect available exports, reconcile definitions and log conflicts. Meetings and generated reports establish reported claims, not underlying transaction accuracy.
- Campaign package: Use the audited baseline and [campaign-package](references/campaign-package.md) for acquisition and reactivation deliverables. Missing data should yield provisional drafts and specific data requests, never invented metrics.

## Evidence rules

Separate verified facts, reported claims, hypotheses and recommendations. Link numeric conclusions to source, extraction date, reporting window and definition. Prefer source exports and preserve unresolved discrepancies.

Match windows and account coverage before classifying sales as organic. No rows can reflect access, sync, account or filter failures. Direct/unknown does not prove word of mouth. Platform-attributed revenue, realized revenue and incremental lift are different measures; expected attribution differences are not automatically tracking faults.

Qualify results with conversion counts and uncertainty. A few conversions do not establish durable winners. Do not infer demographics from names or sum overlapping breakdowns. State cohort maturity and retention denominators.

Keep contact details in source systems and anonymize analytical exports. Preserve supplied product assets and substantiated claims. Do not universalize a client's visual style, product focus or vendor stack.

## Scope and delivery

Default to read-only analysis and drafts. Planning does not authorize changing tracking, budgets, campaigns, flows, enrolling contacts or sending messages. Honor existing explicit authorization without repeatedly asking. For separately authorized execution, act only within its stated audience, content, budget and scope.

Deliver a verdict, source/coverage ledger, definitions, reconciled scorecard, discrepancy log, campaign and reactivation package, and prioritized roadmap with proposed owners, dependencies and acceptance criteria. Include available appropriate exports; mark gaps. Default to 14 days, adapting to the business cycle and user request.

Use the user's chosen output format. Keep client data, credentials and private infrastructure out of reusable skill files and public repositories. No specific connector or third-party skill is required.
