# Evidence collection

Adapt this checklist to actual systems. Record dates, timezone, currency, attribution window/model, extraction time, filters and source status. Missing data is not zero.

## Revenue

Collect daily completed transactions/collected revenue, refunds, discounts, quantities, new/returning customers and priority-offer performance. Distinguish gross sales, net sales, taxes, shipping and receipts. Reconcile averages against documented definitions.

For services include lead, booking, attendance and won-sale stages and conversion lag. For subscriptions include paid activation, recurring revenue changes and paid cohort retention. Do not report pipeline as cash.

Collect anonymized history sufficient for purchase frequency, time to next purchase and mature 30/60/90-day cohorts where relevant. Returning-customer status can refer to purchases before the reporting window, not repeat purchase within it.

## Acquisition and journey

Pull campaign and creative level spend, impressions, clicks, landing visits where available, funnel events, paid conversions, value, CPA and ROAS; include status, targeting, destination and creative previews. Separate brand search, prospecting and existing customers where possible.

Request available age, gender, region, device and placement breakdowns separately, with spend, conversions and suppression notes. Do not assume every breakdown exists or is independently additive.

Review priority landing conversion by source/device, funnel drop-off, support objections, reviews and existing assets. Distinguish observed faults from recommendations.

## Tracking

Identify primary optimization/reporting actions. Check nonpurchase actions counted as purchases, duplicate events, transaction IDs, values/currency and browser/server deduplication where relevant. Compare daily source sales with analytics/platform data, considering timezone, conversion lag, refunds, view-through attribution and overlapping credit.

Do not force attributed totals to equal source revenue. Separate known faults from expected differences. Match periods before historical channel claims.

## Economics

Collect pricing, discounts, inventory/capacity, product/delivery costs, fulfillment, shipping subsidies, payment fees and refunds. Identify missing costs. Calculate contribution before ads with explicit inclusions and exclusions; distinguish it from accounting profit.

Use first-sale contribution as a provisional break-even CAC only for first-sale economics. Repeat-value assumptions need observed cohorts and a payback horizon. Translate allowable CAC into lead cost only with a defensible lead-to-customer rate.

## Reachable database

Collect recency, frequency, value, product/service mix, channel subscription and suppression status, engagement, existing automation membership, recent sends and unresolved support cases. Keep contact details in connected systems; export anonymized IDs.

Count eligible contacts by channel, deduplicate segments and separate previous customers from never-purchased leads. Purchase history alone does not establish messaging eligibility. Audit existing flows and attribution before proposing replacements.

## Outputs

Return available raw exports, a source ledger, definitions, reconciled scorecard and discrepancy log. List missing permissions/data and specific follow-up requests. Link numeric recommendations to evidence and assumptions. Label figures from summaries as reported rather than independently verified.
