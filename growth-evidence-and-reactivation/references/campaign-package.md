# Campaign package

## Acquisition

Recommend an offer, destination, audience/channel hypotheses, creative briefs and budget scenarios bounded by contribution economics and capacity. Separate new-customer acquisition from branded demand capture and existing-customer purchases. Avoid universal spend thresholds and scaling from tiny samples.

Use existing assets where useful. Default to five creative briefs when no count is requested, testing distinct hypotheses. Follow actual brand/placement requirements and preserve exact product appearance when required. Use supported claims, avoid implying knowledge of sensitive personal traits, and check current platform requirements when assessing compliance. Never guarantee approval.

State optimization event, attribution view, evaluation window, conversion lag, budget cap and review/stop criteria. Unreliable tracking/economics require provisional scenarios and launch dependencies.

## Reactivation

Choose model-appropriate segments: one-time buyers due for repeat, lapsed repeat customers, cross-sell candidates, high-value lapsed customers, former subscribers or overdue service customers. Keep never-converted leads separate. Derive lapse thresholds from repeat cadence/contracts; label assumptions.

Apply channel eligibility, suppression and deduplication before counting reachable customers. Identify recent buyers, unresolved support cases, overlapping flows and send frequency conflicts. Specify segment priority and exit-on-conversion. Do not infer messaging eligibility from purchases alone.

Default to three email drafts per priority segment: return invitation, education, then offer/reminder. Adapt cadence and length to context. Include subject, preview, body, CTA, destination, entry/exit rules and timing. SMS is optional and limited to eligible recipients. Do not invent deadlines, replenishment intervals or claims.

Compare no-discount messaging with a margin-supported incentive. Show eligibility, cost and contribution. Audit existing flows before recommending new ones to avoid duplicates and overlapping attribution.

## Measurement and roadmap

Propose a randomized holdout when feasible, with exposure period and sample limitations. Prevent cross-campaign contamination. If a meaningful holdout is infeasible, report descriptive results and limit causal claims.

Track eligible audience, delivery, clicks, paid conversions, reactivated customers, revenue/contribution per eligible customer, unsubscribes and complaints. Separate attributed revenue from incremental lift. Do not judge success on opens alone.

Deliver segment counts/definitions, exclusions, message drafts, flow map, economics, measurement plan and launch checklist. Prioritize material measurement/access issues, economics and eligibility, then drafts and an authorized bounded test. Mark owners as proposed unless assigned. Adapt timing to resources and business cycle.
